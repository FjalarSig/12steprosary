Print guide — not part of the card

Pouch card, ready to print

Two pages below, each cut to a poker-card size (63.5 × 88mm / 2.5 × 3.46in) — page 1 is Icelandic, page 2 is English. Print both onto the same piece of card stock, one per side, so the finished card reads Icelandic on one face and English on the other.

  1. Print at 100% / actual size — not "fit to page" — with no margins.
  2. For duplex printers: flip on the short edge so page 2 lands correctly behind page 1.
  3. Printing single-sided instead: print each page separately, then have a print shop mount or laminate them back-to-back on card stock.
  4. The QR points to 12steprosary.vercel.app — scan it with your phone before running a full batch.
